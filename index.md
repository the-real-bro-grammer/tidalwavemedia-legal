# Money Miner — Privacy Policy

*Effective date: 30 July 2026*

Money Miner is developed by Tidal Wave Media ("we", "us"). This policy explains what
information the game handles.

## The short version

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information about you. Your game
progress stays on your device. The only outside service the game contacts is the
advertising network that delivers the optional rewarded videos you choose to watch.

## Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share: your name, contact information,
location, photos, contacts, or usage analytics. The game requires no account, no sign-in,
and no personal details of any kind.

## Information stored on your device only

Your game progress — levels cleared, stars, coins, items, gear, settings — is saved
locally on your device. It never leaves your device, and uninstalling the game deletes
it. We cannot access, restore, or transfer it, so please note that reinstalling starts a
fresh game.

## Optional rewarded advertising

Money Miner shows **rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one** — for example,
to revive after a failed run or to claim an extra bonus. There are no banner ads, no
pop-up interstitials, and no ads that play without you asking for them. You can play the
entire game without ever watching one.

These videos are delivered by **Unity Ads**, a service of Unity Technologies. To serve a
video, cap how often you see one, and prevent advertising fraud, Unity Ads may process
limited technical information such as your device's advertising identifier, device model,
operating system version, and coarse country. That processing is governed by Unity's own
privacy policy:

- [Unity Privacy Policy](https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy)

**How to limit ad tracking.** On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and
opt out of ad personalisation, under **Settings → Privacy → Ads**. On iOS you can decline
app tracking when prompted, or change it under **Settings → Privacy & Security →
Tracking**. Opting out does not stop the rewarded videos from working — it only limits
personalisation.

## Children

Money Miner is a family-friendly game. We collect no data from anyone, including
children. Advertising is limited to the rewarded videos described above, and where the
game is distributed to a child-directed audience the advertising network is configured to
serve only non-personalised ads.

## Permissions

The game requests no sensitive device permissions. It does not use your camera,
microphone, contacts, location, files, or photos.

## Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date
revised before the change ships in a released build.
