# Money Miner — Privacy Policy

*Effective date: 6 November 2026*

Money Miner is developed by Tidal Wave Media ("we", "us"). This policy explains what
information the game handles.

## The short version

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information about you. Your game progress
stays on your device. The only outside services the game contacts are the advertising network that
delivers the optional rewarded videos you choose to watch, and your app store when you choose to buy
a coin pack.

## Information we do not collect

We do not collect, store on our servers, or share: your name, contact information, location,
photos, contacts, or usage analytics. The game requires no account, no sign-in, and no personal
details of any kind.

## Information stored on your device only

Your game progress — levels cleared, stars, coins, items, gear, settings — is saved locally on
your device. It never leaves your device, and uninstalling the game deletes it. We cannot access,
restore, or transfer it, so please note that reinstalling starts a fresh game.

## Optional rewarded advertising

Money Miner shows **rewarded video ads only when you choose to watch one** — for example, to
revive after a failed run or to claim an extra bonus. There are no banner ads, no pop-up
interstitials, and no ads that play without you asking for them. You can play the entire game
without ever watching one.

These videos are delivered by **Unity Ads**, a service of Unity Technologies. To serve a video,
cap how often you see one, and prevent advertising fraud, Unity Ads may process limited technical
information such as your device's advertising identifier, device model, operating system version,
and coarse country. That processing is governed by Unity's own privacy policy:

- Unity Privacy Policy: <https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy>

**How to limit ad tracking.** On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, and opt out
of ad personalisation, under **Settings → Privacy → Ads**. On iOS you can decline app tracking
when prompted, or change it under **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking**. Opting out does
not stop the rewarded videos from working — it only limits personalisation.

## Optional in-app purchases

You can buy **coin packs** (fixed amounts of in-game coins) with real money. Every coin can also be
earned by playing, and nothing in the game can only be bought with money. Purchases are optional.

Payments are handled entirely by your app store (**Google Play** on Android, the **App Store** on iOS).
We never see or receive your name, payment card, billing address, or account details. The game only
receives a confirmation that the purchase went through, plus an order number, which it keeps on your
device so the same order is never credited twice. That information is not sent to us.

Purchases are processed under your app store's own terms and privacy policy:

- Google Privacy Policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>
- Apple Privacy Policy: <https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/>

Refunds are requested through your app store. You can block or require approval for purchases using
your app store's parental controls or purchase-authentication settings.

## Children

Money Miner is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13.
We collect no data from anyone, including children. Advertising is limited to the rewarded videos
described above.

## Permissions

The game requests no sensitive device permissions. It does not use your camera, microphone,
contacts, location, files, or photos.

## Changes to this policy

If the game's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the effective date revised
before the change ships in a released build.
