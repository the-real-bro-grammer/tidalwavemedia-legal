# Tidal Wave Media — Legal

Public hosting for the legal documents Google Play and the App Store require a live URL for.

| Document | URL |
|---|---|
| Money Miner privacy policy | https://the-real-bro-grammer.github.io/tidalwavemedia-legal/ |

The source of truth for the Money Miner policy lives with the game at
`docs/release/privacy-policy.md`. When that changes, update `index.md` here and revise the
effective date **before** the matching build ships.

Keep this in sync with the Play Console **Data safety** form — a mismatch between the two is
one of the more common review rejections.
